## Volume Split Rationale (4a / 4b)

Volume 4 was originally planned as a single volume spanning Chapters 17–27.
During development — particularly during the Exercise 26 troubleshooting arc
(§26.3.3–§26.3.5.5) — the volume grew past 400 PDF pages, exceeding the
Amazon KDP paperback page limit (~400 pages) and becoming disproportionately
larger than Volumes 1–3.

The split at the **Stage 5 / Stage 6 boundary** was chosen because:

1. **Thematic balance.** 4a is the "make it right" half — concepts, meaning,
   reuse, governance, validation. 4b is the "make it act" half — inference and
   execution. The split mirrors the book's own `K → R → Θ/Φ` arc.
2. **Print viability.** Both volumes now sit comfortably under the KDP limit
   and closer in size to Volumes 1–3.
3. **Natural hand-off.** Chapter 22 ends with *Looking Ahead — Toward Semantic
   Reasoning*; Chapter 23 opens with *From Verification to Discovery*. The
   boundary is already a designed pause.
4. **Forward compatibility.** 4b's back half (Ch. 26–27) is the natural on-ramp
   to Volume 5 (Executable Knowledge Implementation, Ex. 22–36 in the original
   plan). Keeping Stage 7 contiguous with the synthesis preserves that bridge.

Chapter numbering is preserved. No chapters were renumbered. The split is a
container-level change only.
